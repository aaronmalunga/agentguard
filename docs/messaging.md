# AgentGuard: messaging (locked 9 Nov 2026, Aaron)

One source for wording. The app, README, Devpost text, gallery and video use these lines exactly.

| Use | Line |
|---|---|
| **Hook** (video opener; first thing heard) | Your agent just paid. Did you mean it? |
| **Insight** (right after the hook; Devpost "Inspiration") | Authorized isn't the same as intended. |
| **Tagline** (title card, README, Devpost) | PayPal moves the money. AgentGuard checks you meant it. |
| **One-liner** (elevator pitch, profile README) | AgentGuard checks every AI agent payment against what the buyer actually meant, before money moves on PayPal. |

## Word choices (why)
- **"checks", never "proves".** AgentGuard checks intent against the buyer's rules and the evidence; it can't prove
  what someone had in mind.
- **"built on PayPal", never "PayPal AgentGuard".** PayPal Developer Agreement (brief s.2).
- **Hook delivery:** open (decided when the video is planned). Draft: payment sound, "Your agent just paid.", one
  second of silence, "Did you mean it?".
- Hero unchanged since 25 Oct (D1): intent verification before money moves.
